---
name: pptx-deck
description: Make a clean PowerPoint deck with short slides
---
1. Outline first: a title slide, 4 to 8 content slides, one idea per slide, an ending slide (summary or next steps).
2. Titles are statements, not topics. At most 5 bullets per slide, each under 12 words.
3. Create slides with one add per slide (see the built-in PowerPoint recipe). Use "Title Slide" for the first, "Title and Content" for the rest.
4. Validate, then view as text to check order and wording. Offer to adjust tone or length.
