---
name: academic-docx
description: Write an academic-style Word report with headings, citations, captions and a reference list
---
Structure: Title, short abstract, numbered headings (1, 1.1), conclusion, References.
1. Gather sources first with the research skill. Cite in text as (Author, Year) and list every cited source under References in APA style: Author. (Year). Title. Publisher or site. URL
2. Build with officecli batch (see the built-in recipe). Title 20pt bold, headings 14pt bold with spaceBefore 12pt, body 11pt Calibri, justify or left, spaceAfter 6pt.
3. Tables get a bold header row and a caption line above ("Table 1. ..."). Figures get a caption below.
4. Put a page break before References when the heading would otherwise land at the bottom of a page.
5. Verify with the verify-document skill, and respect any page limit the user gave.
