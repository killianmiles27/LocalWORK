---
name: research
description: Answer questions that need evidence from the web, with cited sources
---
1. web_search with 2 to 3 differently worded queries. Prefer official, primary and recent sources.
2. web_fetch at least 3 independent pages. If a page returns almost nothing, move on to the next result.
3. Write down each claim with its source URL and date. Separate what a source says from your own inference, and label the inference.
4. If sources disagree, say so and name both. If you found nothing solid, say that. Never invent a source, number or quote.
5. Finish with a short answer first, then a "Sources" list of the URLs you actually fetched (title, URL).
